Take their qualities, never their subject, palette or layout, so every product still gets its own screen. `unslop-dashboard-after` is the finished version of `examples/dashboard.html`, so when you unslop that example, find your own answer instead of rebuilding it.

What they share: one bright element per screen, quiet chrome around it, a visual that can be explained in one sentence, and headlines placed on space that was left empty for them.

## Where the images and the rules differ

A few details in these images break rules in SKILL.md. Follow the rules, not the images, on these points.

- The hero image shows a revenue stat card with mini bars. Stat cards stay numbers only.
- The finished dashboard writes the overdue count as `07`. Write counts without a leading zero, `7`.
- The meadow footer has crystal shapes, which visuals.md lists as an AI default. They work there because the product is a calm reading app with a soft 3D world. Do not reach for crystals unless the world calls for them.
- The glowing curves of the dashboard chart and the glowing tags in the hero are moment effects. They are allowed once per screen under Moment effects in SKILL.md.

## Living concepts

Live pages by Damir Sokolovsky on [living-ui.dev](https://living-ui.dev). With a browser, open them and play with them before writing a concept. Without one, read the notes. Take how the idea is found and how the object answers, never the object itself.

| Page | Concept | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Typewriter test](https://living-ui.dev/play/typewriter) | A typing test on a real typewriter whose keys are live, headline `Typos stay on paper` in a typewriter face with the word Typos in ribbon red | The old thing the product replaces becomes the hero, the world gives the typeface and the one red, the primary button takes the machine's graphite metal and carries its key as a keycap |
| [Accordion FAQ](https://living-ui.dev/play/accordion) | An FAQ that is a real red accordion, pressing a key opens the bellows into the questions | The component name taken literally, and the component's own job, opening and closing, is what the object does |
| [Vinyl lyrics](https://living-ui.dev/play/vinyl-lyrics) | A record cut by the left edge of the page while the song's notes scroll past as mono text | The object bleeds off the frame so it feels big, and the lyrics are drawn in the product's own material, note names |
| [Roadster park](https://living-ui.dev/play/roadster) | A small multiplayer driving game with the whole interface in pixels | When the page is the game, the interface commits to its world, pixel type and stepped corners, and shows its keys, `Enter to drive` |
| [404](https://living-ui.dev/404) | `This page sank`, the digits half under water in a small reef you can stir by shaking the window | The words of the error taken literally, one plain line says how to play, the reef has its own named colors, and sound waits behind `Hear the water` |
| [Living UI home](https://living-ui.dev) | A two line centered headline over a sea of clouds at dusk, one word in a copper sheen, a waitlist field with a lit ring | Centered works because it is short and sits on calm sky, and the one sheen, the one ring and the clouds share the same warm light |
| [heydamir.design](https://heydamir.design) | A narrow fixed column with the pitch next to a scrolling wall of looping videos, each in a bezel | Rail and wall composition, nested radii on every bezel, a human status line with the local time, one lit button |

More concepts as video on heydamir.design: a glass slab splitting a beam into a spectrum for a light study, a book on a sunlit desk for a landing page, a door glowing in fog for an app that gets you off the feed, a snow globe you shake for a winter cabin, a shredder on a desk for private documents.
