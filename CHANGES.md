# Changes in this adaptation

Adapted from [heydamir/unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky, commit 4652254, under CC BY 4.0. The original credit stays in every file.

The aim is to close the gap between what the skill tells Claude and what Damir's own work does on heydamir.design and living-ui.dev, reviewed on 31 October 2026. Several rules banned techniques his own best pages rely on, and a few of the reference images broke the rules next to them, which gives Claude mixed signals.

## SKILL.md

- New principle: an effect is slop on the chrome and craft inside the moment when the world explains it. New section, Moment effects, with a four part test: physical, from the world, on the moment only, calm.
- Direction gets a sixth line, Concept, for landing pages, empty states and 404s: one object and one verb.
- What good looks like: adds something alive on pages that sell, with dashboards kept calm.
- Color: text roles as one ink at three opacities, layered shadows for floating things, scene tinted shadows over images, the ban on glows and gradient text scoped to the chrome.
- Icons: color allowed inside the moment.
- Copy: adds the constructive half, one turn of phrase from the world, plain instructions for play with the key shown, human status lines.
- Type: the world's own typeface for the headline, variable weights 420 to 450 for body and 600 for buttons, specimen notes as the one uppercase exception, tracking that depends on the family.
- Layout: five compositions for a concept, object stage, bleed, still life from above, artboard and rail and wall.
- Details: pills allowed for buttons and chips, material recipes, chart line glow on dark grids.
- Visuals: objects people can touch are built in code.
- Motion: moment loops, one reward overshoot, cursor light, real physics for the object, `prefers-reduced-motion`, sound only on request.
- Do not: no copying the author's own look onto every product, no playthings on dashboards.
- Checklist: four new or changed lines for moment effects, reduced motion and keys, the concept, and an unslop slop check.

## references

- New living.md: how to find the object and the verb, how to build it in code, how to make it discoverable and accessible, when not to.
- tokens.md: text as one ink with a warm dark example, named moment colors, button details, Inter Tight and a world typeface row, body and button weights, family dependent tracking, larger radii for dialogs and bezels, a Materials section with dialog, quiet button, bezel, cursor light, lit ring and frosted chip recipes, longer motion values and reduced motion.
- signs.md: glow and gradient signs scoped to the chrome, three accessibility signs in Motion, a new Unslop slop group.
- refs.md: where the images and the rules differ, and a Living concepts table that links to the live pages.

## Not changed

The nine passes and their order, the audit and polish modes, the story video and its scripts, effects.md, visuals.md and the examples.
